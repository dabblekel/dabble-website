:: ABOVE ::

Vesuvius, painted for people who bought eruptions the way we buy prints. The bay is calm, the boats are out, the sky is doing something unrepeatable — and the whole composition is arranged so that catastrophe reads as an evening's entertainment.

:: BELOW ::

The tension is the point. It is very hard to make an image of danger that does not, somewhere in the making, become an advertisement for it.
