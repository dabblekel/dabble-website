:: ABOVE ::

Fateful experiences imbue the everyday a sense of magic. These moments happen in the most unexpected of circumstances: the cashier at the grocery store having the same birthday as you, or discovering that your long-lost childhood friend lives next door. 

:: BELOW ::

What if there was a way we could invite more of these moments to catch our attention? Fate Matchbooks was a project that invited participants to seek out the most magical, ordinary experiences of their day, and pass it on to the people around them.
