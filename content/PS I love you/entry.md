:: ABOVE ::

Food is a conduit of love- it distills emotion and care in all its flavors and sets it onto a plate. 

:: BELOW ::

This piece is about love in all its flavors and learning to savor each one. It's about pausing to appreciate small moments of love that appear unannounced, gentle, warm, sometimes imperceptible, and letting the feeling wash over you.
