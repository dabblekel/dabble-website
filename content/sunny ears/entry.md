:: ABOVE ::

The world around us has not been built keeping our ears in mind, and it turns out, most users don't give them much thought either. 

There is mounting evidence that hearing health is one of the most easily modifiable risk factors for preventing neurodegenerative disease later in life. Hearing aids help, but how do we get people to wear them? 

:: BELOW ::

Hearing aid technologies face cultural and systemic barriers to adoption, with only a 62% adherence rate among patients who choose intervention.  This project explores the technologies, behaviors and systems that surround hearing loss, and determined that we can do better. [Citation: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10299666/]
