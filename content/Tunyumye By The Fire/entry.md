:: ABOVE ::

A physical storytelling game that rekindles the warmth, laughter, and wisdom that once filled family evenings around the fireplace...and our loft evenings:) Tunyumye is a Luganda word that means “let’s chat.”
Today, many of our shared moments happen through screens. We can easily spend an evening on our phones or watching TV, each of us in the same room but somewhere else.


:: BELOW ::

Tunyumye is an invitation to put the screens down, gather around the fire, and bring that magic back. A playful way to spark stories, laughter, deeper conversations, and the kind of connection that stays with you long after the evening ends.
