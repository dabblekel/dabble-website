Any number of images (including none). They appear between the two blocks of copy on the
card, each at full width and its own proportions, and never in the swirl. They are shown
in filename order, so name them 01-..., 02-... to control the sequence.

To caption one, put a text file of the same name beside it:

    01-workshop.jpg
    01-workshop.txt      <- one line, prints in small caps under the image

A .txt with no matching image - this file, for instance - is ignored.
