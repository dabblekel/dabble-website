:: ABOVE ::

Tending the Us explores how design can help us remember our role as tenders of the relationships and ecosystems we belong to. Through a series of participatory experiments, including recipe exchanges, shared dinners, storytelling, and objects designed to continue beyond a single interaction, the project investigated how small acts of attention and participation can create reciprocity. 

:: BELOW ::

Across these experiments, five conditions emerged: safety enables participation, attention makes relationship visible, participation creates implication, continuation sustains reciprocity, and care requires vulnerability. Rather than designing connection itself, Tending the Us asks how we might create the conditions for people to notice, participate in, and care for the systems that hold us.
