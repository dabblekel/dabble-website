Anastasha partnered with the Exploratorium to bring Light Play to the Stanford d.school's 20th anniversary exhibition. With the tinkering studio's materials, we built an exhibition that allowed participants to "mess up" and rework each box, inviting analog play for an ever-changing display, and bringing about collective ownership & imagination.
