:: ABOVE ::

Pain is full of nuance and meaning. The 1-10 pain scale ignores it all, reducing a complex experience to a singular point. Particularly in chronic pain contexts, there is a gap between what an individual is able to express and what providers of care are able to understand. 

:: BELOW ::

Illuminate explores what it looks like to give pain a new visual language, one that empowers patient advocacy while providing clinical utility.
