:: ABOVE ::

When Is Now? is a conceptual clock inspired by the experience of Alzheimer's disease. Rather than measuring hours and minutes, it visualizes one's place within the changing seasons, offering time as orientation instead of precision. 

:: BELOW ::

The piece explores how design can preserve presence and dignity when conventional ways of understanding time begin to fade. Learn more: https://dianerhim.me/time-piece-when-is-now
