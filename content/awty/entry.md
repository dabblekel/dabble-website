:: ABOVE ::

Time is a resource in the English language-- something to be spent, saved, wasted, or used wisely. But this way of thinking about time inevitably leads to a scarce and fleeting relationship, where every moment spent is one that you can never get back. How can we learn to relate to it differently?

:: BELOW ::

are we there yet? (2026) is an interactive installation and performance art piece which offers a new approach to the practice of meditation. As participants cross off lines of repeating text on a wall, the sensation of time passing becomes impossible to ignore.
