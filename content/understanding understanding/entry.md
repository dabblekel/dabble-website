:: ABOVE ::

Understanding Understanding is an instrument and process for turning shared perception into art. Two people walk together with a disposable camera, taking turns seeing and scribing so that a shared world starts to surface between them. Back home, they transform what they collected into a blackout poem. The process ends in a small private gallery, so the art and the relationship behind it get witnessed. The participants learn together in this process that understanding someone doesn't mean seeing the same thing, but weaving two ways of seeing into one.
