:: ABOVE ::

Lagu Hantu resists the irresponsibly transient cultures of today's technologies. It follows a counter-model (the musical instrument); living technologies, built for cultural immortality and enduring ritual. It is a participatory sonic archive in the form of a handmade instrument installation that remembers everyone who has ever played it. When someone interacts with it, a bespoke computational algorithm searches an ever-growing archive of every past performance, summoning fragments that once accompanied similar gestures and playing them back through nearby loudspeakers.

:: BELOW ::

We imagine the archive as a ritual rather than a repository, playing music with each other for centuries, and seeing the instrument far outlast us.
