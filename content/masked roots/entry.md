:: ABOVE ::

A 2×4 piece of lumber, transformed.
It is a metaphor for what education risks becoming as AI reshapes learning, a future where the visible gains are real, but something essential is lost beneath them.
The top surface is polished & gleaming, representing the promise that has always anchored schooling: efficiency, skill, employability. The qualities education sells. In an age of AI, these accelerate further. The shine gets brighter.

:: BELOW ::

But underneath, the wooden skull represents the roots that remain: trust, belonging, care, collective learning, the bonds between people that make meaningful learning possible. They are structural. They are also easy to ignore when the top surface looks this good.
The sculpture asks what gets preserved, and what gets quietly displaced, when we optimize for the top.
