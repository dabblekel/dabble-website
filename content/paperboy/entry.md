:: ABOVE ::

In the face of changing media landscapes and local news organizations shuttering around the country, what everyday needs are left unfulfilled? 

Paperboy began as a research question about the role of local news and media in the lives of residents in Monterey County, California. What emerged was a rich picture of old and new mediums meeting ancient and modern problems: How can I be relevant without being perceived? How can I find solace in my neighbors in hard times? How can sending an article mean "I'm thinking of you"?

:: BELOW ::

Through our research, we found that the problem was not what we expected: rather than another news institution competing for attention and headlines, what was truly needed was a way to repair people's social fabric with their neighbors. By shifting our eye to the "noun condition", Paperboy is now something for people to be part of, offering a fresh take on what a news organization should be.
